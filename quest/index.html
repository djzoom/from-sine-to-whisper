About the course

From a Sine Wave to Whisper is a 99-lesson route from mathematical intuition and audio DSP through deep learning to shipping a working system. The goal isn't "knowing which library to call" — it's being able to explain the formula, write the core algorithm yourself, and turn the result into something real.

The first half fills in complex numbers, linear algebra, calculus, probability and audio features; the second half moves on to ASR, music understanding, LLMs and RAG, and MLOps. Every phase has a concrete deliverable, and it all comes together as an Aurora system you can show, talk through in an interview, and keep researching with.

How long does it take? A Monte Carlo simulation over the time each of the 99 lessons takes puts the whole course at about 131 hours — around 6 months at 5 hours a week. Sprint and it's 2–3 months; dip in and out and a year works too. It's not a race: finishing matters more than finishing fast.

Wherever it can, the course lands each idea in code you can run, a plot you can explain and an experiment you can retrace. What you're left with isn't just notes but an engineering base you can grow into a voice assistant, a retrieval system or an audio research prototype.
